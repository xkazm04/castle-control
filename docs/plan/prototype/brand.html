01

The mark

A chat bubble whose top edge is a battlement: the keep and the crowd in one shape. The wall prints in gules; the gate is lit in or, laid a hair out of register so the two inks meet at the jamb. One component (mark.js) draws it everywhere.

04

Chambers

Each module is a chamber of the keep, drawn in its ink. The plain function always stands beside the brand name; the ids stay lottery, moderator, visuals and studio.

Chamber 1 · lotteryThe Lot Chamber · lottery manager

Seal the roll, wait for the public round, cast the lots. The writ (proof bundle) lets anyone recompute the result.

Chamber 2 · moderatorThe Gatehouse · AI moderator

The moat is the chat delay, AutoMod holds wait at the portcullis, Shield Mode raises the drawbridge.

Chamber 3 · visualsThe Beacons · contribution visuals

Gifts light beacon fires. The kindling curve maps contributions to blaze; test fire before you go live.

Chamber 4 · studioThe Map Room · Extension studio

One reviewed Extension, edited as data. Sand table, then muster, then proclaimed.

06

Voice

A steady castle steward: courteous, brief, plain. The castle is the frame, never the joke, so it works for a cosy art stream, a speedrun or a variety channel.

We say

  • Watching: it says what it would do, and does nothing.
  • Three holds at the portcullis.
  • Here is a writ. Nothing changes until you seal it.
  • The moat buys seconds, not magic.

We don't

  • Promise what Twitch cannot do, or replay events a dropout missed.
  • Use a castle word without its plain function beside it.
  • Borrow platform identity: no Twitch logo, glitch mark or purple.
  • Pass stylised art off as a screenshot.
