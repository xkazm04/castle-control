# The Lit Keep

## Philosophy
The app is a place: the streamer stands in their own keep, and the Steward carries their words from room to room. The first screen is a printed cross-section of the castle. Its four chambers are live rooms, and a watch tower holds the chat consensus. Each room shows its real state at a glance, for example "3 held" or "Pillar of fire". When you speak, the camera moves into the right room and the agent drafts its work there, in front of you.

## How it stays readable at scale
- Each room shows one large word and one plain line; detail lives one level down.
- Inside a room the keep stays faintly visible. The breadcrumb, the 1–4 locator and Esc lead back.
- All the data is reachable:
  - every lot as a mark in the urn;
  - the 420 chat lines replaying, marked Pass, Hold or Bar;
  - 60 offerings on a timeline;
  - 54 hexes;
  - the rules, the drill and the gate log.

## The wow moment
Say "draft a question draw". The keep zooms into the Lot Chamber and a dashed writ inks in. Say "make it 5 winners": the 3 is struck in gules and the 5 is written beside it. The proposal waits in the seal slot. Holding the red button stamps a wax seal. The seal is a real SHA-256 that anyone can recompute.

## Known limits
- Dropped on purpose: menus, settings pages and forms. Setup appears only inside a room.
- The Steward is simulated and understands only fixed phrases. "Hold to speak" types the first suggestion.
- Map tiles are generated, because the sample has none. Gift weighting (1 gift = 100 bits) is illustrative.
